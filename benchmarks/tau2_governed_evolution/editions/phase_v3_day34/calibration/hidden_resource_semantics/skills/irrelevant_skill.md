# CALIBRATION ONLY — Payment Accuracy Procedure

## Bind and report payments accurately

- Before confirming a payment, verify that each selected payment method belongs
  to the correct customer and that the proposed charge matches the transaction.
- Use only payment identifiers and balances observed from the current profile;
  do not invent a method or claim that an unavailable method was charged.
- After a payment succeeds, report the completed charge and booking accurately.
  If a tool rejects a payment, state that outcome without claiming completion.

This matched-quality payment Skill does not teach global allocation, resource
irreversibility, certificate prioritization, or loss of unused value. It is for
Irrelevant calibration only and must not enter a Learner or evolution memory.
