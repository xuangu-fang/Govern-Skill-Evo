# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is often the strongest statement justified by the question, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".
- When an item asks for the strongest provable statement, the correct strength, or an equivalence, and one option asserts that a stronger result than the remaining options can be proved, treat that meta-option as a serious candidate. Compare the substantive options: choose the meta-option if the best concrete option is only a weaker corollary, a restricted or special case, or an unverified strengthening, and no concrete option visibly supplies the complete strongest or equivalence statement. Do not choose a concrete option merely because it is detailed, familiar, or apparently safe, and do not choose the meta-option from wording alone when a concrete option visibly gives the full supported conclusion.
- Preserve the contrast with items lacking such a meta-option: if the concrete options are exhaustive and one is the most specific supported classification, keep that concrete option.

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.
- In options about consistency, separate the claim that a phenomenon is consistent from any added claim about what happens under a further axiom or theory (for example, an additional axiom such as WUA). Do not infer "no model of the further axiom contains the phenomenon" from the mere consistency of the phenomenon without that axiom; evaluate positive and negative compatibility claims separately.
- When two options differ only by such a compatibility clause, prefer the option whose modal direction is actually supported: consistency of P together with the additional axiom is a different and often stronger claim than consistency of P alone, and it is not refuted by the existence of a model of P outside the axiom.
- For oscillatory averaging or variation operators, smooth dependence of each transformed function on a parameter does not by itself imply a uniform L^p operator bound over all Schwartz functions. If an option asserts boundedness on one range of p and unboundedness above a threshold, treat that cutoff as an extra claim requiring a visible cancellation or high-frequency estimate. Without such a mechanism, do not split the range; compare the exact norm and choose the option whose global range is supported rather than inserting a guessed threshold.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Output the final answer as the single option label only.
