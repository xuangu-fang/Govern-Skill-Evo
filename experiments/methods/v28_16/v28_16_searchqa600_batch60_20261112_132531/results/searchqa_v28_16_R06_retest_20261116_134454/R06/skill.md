# Question Answering Skill

- **Answer only the expression that fills the clue's open slot.** Do not append extra identifying words—such as a category label, location, or postmodifier—that the clue already supplies, except words that belong to the established name or term. If the clue says 'this vitamin' or 'this market', give the value/modifier alone: 'C' or 'the bear', not 'Vitamin C' or 'bear market'. If it says 'these exiles of Poker Flat' or 'this state university at Superior', give 'The Outcasts' or 'University of Wisconsin', not the full title/campus name that repeats the supplied postmodifier. Keep an article only when it is part of that name; do not append parenthetical glosses, test or code names, acronym expansions, explanatory alternatives or exhaustive secondary lists; when a passage gives a primary item and then occasional alternatives, answer the primary item alone. When the clue names a tribute or cover act, answer the original act alone rather than the appended category phrase. When the clue is an incomplete proper name with a blank inside or at the start, complete the conventional full name rather than returning only the missing token. For an unquoted work title followed by a descriptive subtitle or title tail, use the short core title when the clue already describes the work; this does not override the quoted-title rule.
  - “Hydrogen bomb” not “Hydrogen bomb (Ivy Mike)” (clue: bomb type)
  - “LPGA” not “LPGA (Ladies Professional Golf Association)” (clue: organization Hall of Fame)
  - Expected 'U2' not 'U2 tribute band' (clue: tribute act)
  - Expected 'Funk & Wagnalls' not 'Funk' (clue: incomplete proper name)
  - Expected 'Studio 60' not 'Studio 60 on the Sunset Strip' (clue: described work title)
- **For a generic class, answer the broad head term.** For a series on these, do not add an unrequested subtype modifier: 'Trains', not 'Passenger trains'; 'a balloon', not 'Speech balloon'.
- **Answer the generic term itself for a type clue.** When the clue asks for a generic term or type, such as 'this term for…' or 'one of these birds', answer the generic term itself rather than a longer proper name or title that contains it; use the specific name only when the clue asks for the named event or work itself, not a type. Phrase these type answers as an indefinite singular where natural; this is the exception to the general no-article preference above, even when the passage's specific name has no article.
  - Expected 'a transit' not 'Transit of Venus' (clue: 'term for…')
  - Expected 'a falcon' not 'Maltese Falcon' (clue: bird type)
- **For a person answer, use the common identifying name.** Include a prenominal honorific that the passages present as part of the standard name and the clue does not supply (e.g., 'Sir' or 'Lord'), but do not add an optional middle initial or repeat a role title the clue supplies ('president', 'governor') when the passages also give a shorter common form. When the clue already supplies a first name, answer the remaining surname, even when the passage gives the fuller name. Keep a regnal numeral or inseparable name element that distinguishes the person or play character; treat it as part of the name, not an optional expansion. If the passages consistently use a fuller name and no shorter form appears, keep the fuller form; this exception is only for optional expansions and not for a first name the clue supplies.
  - Expected 'Arthur' not 'Chester A. Arthur' (clue: first name supplied)
  - Expected 'Richard III' not 'Richard' (clue: regnal numeral distinguishes)
  - Expected 'Sir Isaac Newton' not 'Isaac Newton' (clue: name honorific)
  - Expected 'Lyndon Johnson' not 'Lyndon B. Johnson' (clue: optional middle initial)
- **Use the plain spelling shown in the clue or supporting passage.** Do not import diacritics absent from those texts. When a passage presents an acronym expansion as separate words in a title or heading, use that form rather than a hyphenated variant; this is limited to the passage-supported expansion form.
  - Expected 'Vaclav Havel' not 'Václav Havel'
  - Expected 'Picture in Picture' not 'picture-in-picture'
- **Quoted-title boundary: include a needed premodifier, or complete a missing head.** If the quoted word is the head noun and the missing part is a premodifier, include the full title head: for clue 'this Express', answer 'The Polar Express'; keep articles that are part of the established title. If the clue is only a quoted title fragment with a leading ellipsis, answer the missing head word or phrase that completes the title, not the performer or author associated with it: for '...on my mind', answer 'Georgia', not 'Ellie Goulding'; if several titles contain the fragment, choose the title ending in the quoted fragment and supply its missing head.
- **When the clue consists only of proper names (people or places), answer their shared larger location.** With no predicate, answer the shared state, country, or larger location the passages assign to them, not a relationship label or a full definition. If the clue contains a predicate, event, abbreviation, or quoted title fragment, use that explicit cue rather than this place rule.
  - Expected 'Monaco' not 'sisters' (clue: only 'Princess Caroline & Princess Stephanie')
