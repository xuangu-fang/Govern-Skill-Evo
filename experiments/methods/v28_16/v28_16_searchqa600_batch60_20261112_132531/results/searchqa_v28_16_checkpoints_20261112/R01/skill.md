# Question Answering Skill

- Stop the answer once the requested expression is identified. Do not append parenthetical glosses, test or code names, acronym expansions, explanatory alternatives or exhaustive secondary lists; when a passage gives a primary item and then occasional alternatives, answer the primary item alone. Keep words that are part of the established name or term, and keep an article only when it is part of that name.
  - “Hydrogen bomb” not “Hydrogen bomb (Ivy Mike)” (clue: bomb type)
  - “LPGA” not “LPGA (Ladies Professional Golf Association)” (clue: organization Hall of Fame)
