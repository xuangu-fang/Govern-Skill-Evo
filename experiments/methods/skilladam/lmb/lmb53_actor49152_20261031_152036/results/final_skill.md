# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is the strongest statement the stated hypotheses actually justify, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".
- Fix the required form from the stem before weighing content: a "strongest statement" stem wants the sharpest claim consistent with the hypotheses, an "equivalent to" stem wants a two-directional option, and a "complete classification" stem wants the exhaustive list. An option in the wrong form is wrong even when its content sounds true.
- Eliminate mechanically: an option that contradicts a quantity, range, inequality, or assumption written explicitly in the stem cannot be correct. If two options both survive, prefer the one that states the result plainly and completely over one carrying an extra proviso such as "if additionally", "provided that", or "in the special case".

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Work through the comparison first, then put only the single option label inside the answer tags.
