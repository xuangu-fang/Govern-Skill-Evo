responses. Commit unchanged proposals only to isolated copies of original Memory;

historical evidence and prior knowledge. Priorities: heat plate 13-success versus

scope-external comparisons; observation versus absence/causal inference; evidence
sufficiency and growth. These are diagnostic cases, not estimates of reliability.
