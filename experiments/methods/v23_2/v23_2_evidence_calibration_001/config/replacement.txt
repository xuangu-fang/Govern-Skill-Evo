Examine each Update relative to the Parent Skill. Use applicable historical edit–behavior responses and current evidence to determine both what should change and how the resulting strategy, conditions, and strength should be expressed. Support for an improvement goal does not establish a specific strategy, and evidence against an original strategy does not establish its replacement.

For every retained or revised Update, match the scope and force of its material requirements to the evidence or problem-grounded hypothesis supporting them. A reason to change one requirement does not by itself justify additional restrictions or changes to necessary task-completion conditions. Substantial revisions and novel strategies are allowed: identify the testable hypothesis and expected behavioral change in the existing reasons and, where useful, checks, without presenting expected benefits as observed effects.

Keep the behavioral instructions clear and actionable. Preserve otherwise valid requirements and check executability using information and actions available to the Actor at decision time.
