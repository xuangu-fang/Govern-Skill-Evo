# Question Answering Skill
- For an explicit placeholder ('multi-this,' 'the negative rate of this since 1933'), answer only the filling term, not the completed compound or longer statistic.

- When the clue already identifies the item by a specific name, stop at that name: no parenthetical translation, acronym expansion, actor/portrayer, or clue-supplied category. Answer 'LPGA' (not the expanded name), 'Conan' (not 'Conan (played by ...)'), and 'Anastasia' (not 'Anastasia Romanov' when the clue calls her a Romanov). For a person, use the name form supported by the passage sentence stating the clue's relation, not the most complete-looking variant or page title. If a shortened and formal given name both appear, use the form in that relation sentence; do not expand a familiar form merely because a fuller form appears elsewhere. If passages identify the person only by surname (or title plus surname), keep that bare form and do not add a first name from outside the passages. If first+last and a middle-initial or Jr./Sr. variant both appear, use bare first+last unless the longer form is consistently attached to that person. Do not repeat a role or title supplied by the clue (e.g., 'this Polish count') before the personal name; keep a standard honorific only when consistently attached as part of the established English name (e.g., 'Sir Francis Drake'). Keep initials or honorifics belonging to the established name, but do not invent one absent from the evidence.

- When the clue asks for a named place, building, or landmark without supplying its name, use the established English/common name from the passage's identifying sentence, not a foreign original, translation, or current sponsor/renamed form: if a passage says 'X in English [is] Y,' answer Y ('Pitti Palace,' not 'Palazzo Pitti'); if a historic/possessive name and a current corporate renaming both appear and the clue identifies the venue by address or features, use the historic established name ('Grauman's Chinese Theater,' not 'TCL Chinese Theatre'). Keep a state/city qualifier that is part of the identifying name ('Anchorage, Alaska'). Do not apply this when the clue asks for the current name.

- If the question is only a bare name or a list of capitalized names with no interrogative or relation word, do not answer with a descriptive sentence or a generic role. Return the single shared enclosing entity—especially the state, country, or other place that contains or is associated with all the listed items—and stop at that entity. If the prompt has a relation word, a quoted fragment, or wordplay enumeration, treat it as a different case.

- Match the answer's specificity to what the clue asks: follow the requested noun category. For 'this type of chewing gum,' return the specific variety, not 'modern chewing gum'; for 'the alliance of these Chinese businesses,' return 'laundries,' not its proper name. For a broad category, description, nickname, or quoted generic label ('this ... fish', 'shaped like one of these', 'nicknamed this', 'this comic 'network''), answer the specific underlying term the clue identifies, not a broader category, and stop before a passage's added narrowing modifier such as a species, technology, color, or category noun (e.g., 'lamprey' not 'sea lamprey', 'balloon' not 'hot air balloon' or 'speech balloon', 'eagle' not 'White Eagle', 'Bull Moose' not 'Bull Moose Party', 'SCTV' not 'SCTV Network'). Do not add an evidence qualifier that narrows a requested base term—answer 'C' for 'this vitamin' and 'trains' for a series featuring the Daylight and 20th Century Limited, not 'vitamin C' or 'passenger trains'—and do not append a fuller title merely because a passage contains it. Apply the same check to 'this kind/variety/organization': answer the member of the requested category, not a broader or enclosing entity, and keep a qualifier only when the clue asks for that established name, such as 'South Sea Bubble'; do not apply the quoted-label rule when the quoted word is itself part of the requested title, as with a wordplay-altered Shakespeare title.

- When the clue already supplies the category noun or institutional suffix (for example 'this gland,' 'this missile,' or 'this Louisiana univ.'), answer the identifying base name without repeating that supplied category or suffix: 'thyroid,' 'Pershing,' and 'Grambling,' not 'thyroid gland,' 'Pershing missile,' or 'Grambling State University.' Keep a category word only when it is part of the established identifying name, as with 'Mediterranean Sea' or 'German Shepherd.'
