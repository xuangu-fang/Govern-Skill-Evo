# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is often the strongest statement justified by the question, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case. When the question asks for the strongest provable statement and one option explicitly asserts that a stronger result can be proved, make that option the provisional choice as soon as you identify it; do not treat it merely as a last resort after accepting a universal-looking concrete option. A concrete option displaces the provisional choice only if you prove that it is the strongest justified conclusion under all stated hypotheses and quantifiers, using any converse, endpoint, or injectivity checks that the options require. If the stronger-result option is inconsistent with the stated hypotheses, or an exact concrete strongest theorem is established, choose the concrete option; otherwise retain the stronger-result option when the comparison remains unresolved.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Output the final answer as the single option label only.
