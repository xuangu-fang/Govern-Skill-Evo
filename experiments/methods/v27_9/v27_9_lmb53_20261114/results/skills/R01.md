# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is often the strongest statement justified by the question, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".
- When a question asks for the strongest, most complete, most general, or exact statement, classification, or characterization—including a "which statement is valid" prompt whose options differ in strength—treat an option that says a remaining substantive option is correct but a stronger result can be proven as a live candidate. Compare the substantive option you favor with the conclusion the question actually requests. Select the meta-option when that substantive option gives only an existence claim for one object, lower regularity, a special case, a restricted family, a one-way implication, or another weaker or less complete conclusion, and the meta-option's claim of a stronger result is compatible with the stated hypotheses and quantifiers. Do not select the meta-option merely because it is present. If a substantive option already states the full theorem or exact requested classification, or if the asserted stronger result conflicts with the hypotheses or quantifiers, prefer the substantive option.

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Output the final answer as the single option label only.
