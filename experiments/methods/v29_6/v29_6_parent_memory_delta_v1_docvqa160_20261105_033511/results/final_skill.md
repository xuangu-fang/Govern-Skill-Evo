# DocVQA Skill

## Visual Evidence Discipline
- Read the document carefully before answering.
- Prefer the smallest exact text span that answers the question.
- When the question asks for a single scalar or identifier (an amount, number, date, name, or code), return one minimal contiguous span that directly answers it. If the document shows the same value in more than one notation, such as a spelled-out amount followed by a numeric or symbolic restatement, do not concatenate the representations or append a parenthetical equivalent; keep the form that occupies the requested label/value position. Preserve exact units and printed punctuation that belong to the selected span. This rule limits redundant equivalent renderings, not legitimate parenthetical components of a proper name.
- For a label-like number or code (abstract number, page number, item/entry number, account digits) rather than a measured quantity, inspect what sits immediately before the digits. If a hyphen, dash, bullet, or dot leader separates the digits from a name or label in the surrounding layout, answer with the digits alone without that separator and do not treat it as a minus sign, range marker, or subtraction. If the same digits appear both separated and unseparated, prefer the rendering that occupies the requested field's value position.
- Keep a leading sign only when visible context marks the value as genuinely signed (a loss or deficit, temperature, offset, coordinate, or a field label indicating a negative value), and keep the sign attached to the digits. Retain punctuation that belongs to the field rather than the layout, such as units with their printed period, quotation marks around a slogan, a comma and suffix inside a personal name, and AM/PM markers. Confine this separator exclusion to a leading separator attached directly to identifier digits; do not strip other punctuation, trim trailing periods, or shorten names or multi-word answers.
- For a numeric value in a cell or labeled field (including table entries and invoice amounts), include a unit or symbol only when it is visibly printed as part of that value span. When the question or a column/row heading supplies the unit and the field contains only the number, return the number alone: do not prepend or append the supplied unit. Preserve the value's printed digit grouping, decimal mark, and any sign that belongs to it, and keep an attached symbol or unit exactly. Do not append a unit merely because the question asks for that kind of quantity, and do not strip a symbol that is visibly part of the printed span.
- When the question asks for a person's title, return the title/role span only; do not append an employer, organization, department, or location that appears after the title as a separate affiliation field, even if printed on the same line or after a comma. Keep genuinely multi-word role titles and any component visibly internal to the role. If the question asks for the affiliation or full position, include the organization as requested.
- When a question asks which brand, service, product, or organization is advertised, offered, or named, identify the distinctive display name or brand in the layout rather than every word printed near it. If that name is set off as a logo, heading, or standalone field from a longer category, legal-form, department, location, or descriptor line, return the name span alone and do not append the separate descriptor even when it describes the same entity; for such questions this display-name scope takes precedence over the general guidance to preserve a full proper-name span. Keep the full span only when the question asks for the full legal or company name or an affiliation, or when the extra words are visibly internal to the name itself rather than a separate descriptor. Preserve the printed capitalization, symbols, and multi-word components of the selected span, and do not append the question's category wording as part of the answer.
- When the question asks for a course, program, event, or other named offering and also specifies a schedule, session, day, or format, first locate the complete entry that matches all of those conditions. If a compact schedule/session/section designation is printed inside the same cell, line, or entry boundary as the offering title, treat it as part of the entry and return the full printed span, preserving its capitalization, punctuation, and internal spacing; such an internal designation is not a separate descriptor to strip under the display-name scope rule, and this inclusion takes precedence over the general preference for the smallest span. Do not append such a designation when it belongs to a neighboring entry or to a separate field the question does not request; in that case return the requested title or name span alone.
- When the selected span is a short printed heading, stamp slogan, title, or date range containing apostrophes, quotation marks, or dashes, reproduce the visible punctuation and spacing in that span rather than applying automatic typographic normalization. If an apostrophe or quotation mark is attached to a word or numeral without a space in the document, do not insert a space before or after it; if the visible mark is a straight apostrophe or a short hyphen, do not replace it with a curly apostrophe or a longer dash, and use the longer or curly form only when that form is visibly printed. For a date range, keep the printed dash form instead of converting between hyphen and en/em dash. Leave the selected words unchanged; this character-copy check is not a reason to shorten a multi-word name or to add punctuation not visible in the span.
- When several nearby strings look similar, choose the one whose surrounding labels or layout best match the question.

## Exact Answer Discipline
- Copy names, numbers, and dates exactly from the document whenever possible.
- Prefer direct extraction over paraphrase.
- Before finalizing, compare the answer against nearby alternatives and keep the best-supported exact span.
