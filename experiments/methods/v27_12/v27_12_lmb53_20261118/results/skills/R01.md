# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is often the strongest statement justified by the question, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case. Before committing to a concrete choice, check whether a choice is a meta-statement saying that another listed choice is correct while a stronger result can be proved. Treat that meta-statement as a substantive mathematical answer, not as an evasive non-answer: it can be true even when a concrete listed statement is also true, because the stronger theorem need not appear among the choices. When the question asks for the valid, strongest, or complete statement, and the concrete choices supply only partial, weakened, restricted, or non-exhaustive versions, and no concrete choice already states the full stronger conclusion, submit the stronger-result meta-choice. Do not apply this preference when the only meta-choice merely concedes that a weaker statement holds, or when a concrete choice itself is the exact strongest result with all required hypotheses and equality, converse, or exhaustive-clause conditions present.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Output the final answer as the single option label only.
