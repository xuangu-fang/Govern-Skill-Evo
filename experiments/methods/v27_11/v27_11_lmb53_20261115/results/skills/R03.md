# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is often the strongest statement justified by the question, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- In a classification or complete-list question, separate the forward list of possible objects from a converse clause saying that every parameter in the stated range actually occurs. Accept the converse only when the hypotheses or a stated theorem provide an object for each such parameter; otherwise choose the version that gives the exact list without the unsupported addition that every such parameter occurs.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.
- Match each candidate's scope to the exact object in the question. If the question asks about a full gradient, an unrestricted function class, or an underlying tree or graph, then a rival using one component, a frequency- or support-restricted subclass, a universal cover, or a decorated variant is a restricted or auxiliary statement, not the unrestricted strongest answer. Prefer the restricted form only when the question itself asks for that restriction or when the theorem is explicitly stated for that object.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Output the final answer as the single option label only.
