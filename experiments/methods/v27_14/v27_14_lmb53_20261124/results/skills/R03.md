# Live Mathematical MCQ Heuristics

## Option Comparison
- Compare all options before committing. The correct choice is often the strongest statement justified by the question, while nearby distractors are weaker, overstrong, or miss an equality case.
- When a question asks for the strongest statement or strongest provable result and one choice defers to the other choices while asserting that a stronger result can be proven, do not dismiss it merely because it does not state the theorem. Evaluate that deferring choice as a substantive candidate. Before selecting a concrete alternative, check whether that alternative is only a restricted form of the claimed stronger statement, such as one decomposition type, a local instead of a global conclusion, a fixed exponent instead of a range, or an added support or regularity condition. Also check whether every concrete alternative is too weak, too strong, or misses an exact condition. If no concrete alternative is demonstrably the exact strongest theorem under the hypotheses, select the deferring choice.
- Track exact quantifiers such as "there exists", "for every", "if and only if", and "exactly when".
- For choices that differ by an exponent, a norm, or a "for every epsilon" family in a divergence or blow-up condition, first determine which statement implies which under the stated time interval and hypotheses. Do not select a formally stronger family merely because it contains more quantifiers or a smaller exponent. If the hypotheses justify only a single critical exponent or endpoint, that single statement is the strongest provable one.

## Theorem-Level Precision
- Check whether an option weakens the conclusion by dropping a characterization, equality clause, or full equivalence.
- Check whether an option overstates the theorem by upgrading regularity, removing scale restrictions, or changing an existential statement into a universal one.

## Hypotheses
- Verify the hypotheses and domain carefully. Distractors often keep the theorem shape but alter the required assumptions.
- Pay close attention to equality cases, extremal conditions, and whether a result applies to the full family or only a restricted subfamily.

## Final Answer
- Output the final answer as the single option label only.
