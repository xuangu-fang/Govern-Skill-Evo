### all-n-sign-equivalence

**冻结的 Original**

When the stem asks for an equivalence involving a fixed sign for all sufficiently large integers, test whether the offered condition is really all-integer. For Fourier coefficients or other multiplicative arithmetic functions, signs at primes do not determine signs at prime powers or composite indices, and equality of absolute values or a merely nonzero ratio does not give a nonnegative product. A prime-only, absolute-value-only, one-way, or nonzero-ratio condition is therefore only partial. If no concrete choice states the all-integer sign condition together with the needed positivity and a stronger-result choice is present, select the stronger-result choice.
