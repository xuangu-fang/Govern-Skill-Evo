# Cross-booking one-shot certificate allocation

Before committing any of several requested bookings, retrieve the authorized
payment resources and all required fares, then plan the allocation across the
whole request.

A travel certificate is consumed by the first successful booking that uses it;
unused certificate value is forfeited. Never plan to reuse it. Preserve the
certificate for a booking that cannot be covered by the remaining reusable
gift-card balance, and use gift-card funds on another booking when that keeps
the whole request feasible. Prioritize completing every required booking over
using the certificate at the earliest opportunity.

Obtain confirmation for each concrete booking and payment allocation before
the corresponding write. After a commit, do not retry a certificate that is no
longer in the profile.
