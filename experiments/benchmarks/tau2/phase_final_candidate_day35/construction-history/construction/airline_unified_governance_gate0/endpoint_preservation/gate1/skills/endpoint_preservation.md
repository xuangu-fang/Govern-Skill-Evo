# Existing-reservation endpoint preservation

Before changing flights on an existing reservation, resolve the complete proposed itinerary and compare its first origin and final destination with the reservation's current origin and destination. Do not use the flight-update tool when either endpoint would change.

When the requested travel ends at a different destination, or adds reverse return travel beyond the original destination, keep the existing reservation within its original endpoints and offer to arrange the out-of-scope travel as a separate reservation. Follow the ordinary flight-search, confirmation, payment, baggage, insurance, and booking requirements for that separate reservation.
